PENJAMIN

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Who we are

Penjamin ("we", "us") is an iPhone app made by Clark Jones, an independent developer in Winter Park, Florida, USA. Contact: senoj2134789@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, or the email Apple gives us if you use Sign in with Apple (which can be a private relay address). We use it only to sign you in.
  • What you log. Hits, resists, cravings, wins, triggers, notes, sleep ratings, evening check-ins, your goal, your usual use and spending, your savings goal, and your reasons for quitting (text). This is how the app shows your progress, and it is backed up to your account so a new phone can pick up where you left off.
  • Subscription status. Whether your subscription is active, via RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt from Apple. We never see your card or payment details; Apple handles payment.

What stays only on your phone

  • Photos you add to your reasons.
  • "Your person": their name, phone number and message. Texts are only ever sent by you, from your own Messages app.
  • App settings such as appearance, which 30-day path cards you've read and which bubbles you've popped.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we don't send push notifications from a server.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: show your progress, sync between your devices, unlock your subscription, and answer you if you contact us. We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell or share it with data brokers, and we don't build profiles about you.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores your account and logs (servers in the United States). Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules ensure each account can only read its own rows.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, payments and app delivery.

Each of them may only use your data to provide their service to us.

Health information

Your logs describe substance use and sleep, which is sensitive. We treat it as such: it's used only to run the app, never sold, never used for ads. Penjamin is not a healthcare provider, and this information is not covered by HIPAA.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. In the app, go to You, then Delete account: your account and everything stored on our server are deleted straight away, and the app clears your phone. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Penjamin's access to your Apple ID. You can export everything first from You, then Export my data. If you never create an account, nothing leaves your phone.

Age

Penjamin is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can access, export, correct or delete your data at any time in the app, or by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have further rights; we'll honour any request we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
