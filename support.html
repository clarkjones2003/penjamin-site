Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Penjamin. Or in the app: You, then Manage subscription.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app: You, then Delete account. This removes your account and everything you logged from our server straight away, and clears it from your phone. Deleting the app alone does not cancel a subscription.
I slipped. Did I lose my progress?
No. Your level, your bubbles and every block on your board stay. Only the live timer restarts.
Can I export my data?
Yes: You, then Export my data. You get a file with everything you've logged.
